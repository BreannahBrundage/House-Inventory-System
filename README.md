# House-Inventory-System

The Home Inventory System is designed to help users keep track of the belongings they own and where they are placed in their home. Users will be able to add items, organize them into different categories, and their specific location, such as a room, closet, cabinet, or storage container. The system would allow users to easily search through the entire inventory to quickly locate an item. Users could even create containers and place items in each container along with item descriptions and where they are now located. The primary goal is to enable users to map their entire household inventory to make finding and organizing their belongings easier. 

The value of the system comes from an idea I have kept in mind for a long time. I have always believed that an inventory system for my home would be a lifesaver. I struggle with an "out of sight, out of mind" lifestyle caused by ADHD. It becomes an issue for me to remember all the items I have in my home, and if I cannot remember what I own, then I have trouble locating it. If I place an item in a storage container, I may forget about it altogether. This can make finding specific items difficult and makes me feel disorganized beyond what I can control. I am not the only person who struggles with this issue. Therefore, an inventory system could be a perfect solution for individuals like me. It provides a way for users to view their entire inventory and have quick access to where an item is located when needed. I believe a house inventory system would be an amazing project that I would be interested in working on for the remainder of the semester.
